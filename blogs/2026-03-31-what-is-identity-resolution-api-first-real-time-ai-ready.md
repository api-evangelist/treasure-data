What Is Identity Resolution? Identity resolution is the process of matching and merging fragmented customer data — from dozens of systems, devices, and channels — into a single, accurate profile for each real person. It's how a Customer Data Platform (CDP) turns "anonymous visitor on mobile," "email subscriber," and "loyalty member in-store" into one unified customer.
