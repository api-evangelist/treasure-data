When Customer Data Platforms (CDPs) first emerged, critics dismissed them as glorified Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems or redundant data warehouses. They missed the bigger picture: static customer records weren't enough to power real-time personalized experiences. History proved us right, and first-party data unification became foundational to modern customer engagement.
