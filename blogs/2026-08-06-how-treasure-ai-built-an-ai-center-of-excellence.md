Two years ago, Treasure AI set out to be deliberate about AI from the start. We formed a cross-functional AI committee, drafted our first AI policy, and mapped departmental use cases so adoption fit each function rather than being imposed on it. We built an AI champions program to carry that thinking into every team, not just IT.
