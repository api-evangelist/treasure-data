What Is Customer 360? Customer 360 (also called a customer 360 view) is a unified, real-time view of every customer — built by connecting data from every system, channel, device, and interaction into a single profile. It's the foundational concept behind Customer Data Platforms (CDPs) : one customer, one profile, one truth.
